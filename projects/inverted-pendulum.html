Modeling & Control of an Inverted Pendulum

A cart–pole study that connects nonlinear dynamics, swing-up planning and upright stabilization in simulation.

Overview

Developed a cart–pole model in MATLAB/Simulink and Simscape Multibody, then designed a controller that moves the system from a hanging configuration to the upright equilibrium.

Approach

  • Derive and validate the cart–pole dynamics.
  • Use an energy-based swing-up policy to build the required pendulum energy.
  • Switch to LQR state feedback near the upright equilibrium.

Tools & topics

  • MATLAB
  • Simulink
  • Simscape Multibody
  • LQR
  • State-space modeling

What this demonstrates

The project is the control foundation for a physical inverted-pendulum build, where actuator limits, friction and travel constraints can be compared with the simulation.
